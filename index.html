Purpose Path

Coaching & walking retreat · Málaga, Spain · Sep 2026

100%Remaining places booked

Selling out a high-ticket retreat with a DM-first Meta funnel.

Overview

Purpose Path is a four-day coaching and walking retreat in the hills above Málaga, run by three coaches. With the October 2026 retreat approaching and places still open, they brought me in on a one-month trial to close the gap before the booking deadline.

The problem

Their Instagram was building trust, but organic reach alone wasn’t filling a €2,599 retreat in time. There was no paid acquisition, no landing page for cold traffic, and no system for tracking which leads actually turned into founder calls and bookings.

The solution

  • DM-first Meta campaign: ran ads on Meta’s Messages objective straight into Instagram DMs, matching the “DM BOOK” call to action the audience already knew, so paid and organic pushed the same behaviour.
  • Four creative angles: the three coaches, a creator’s UGC testimonial, a price-to-value reframe, and a final-week scarcity push. Weak concepts were paused and budget moved to the winners.
  • Tight targeting: 35 to 60 year olds across the UK, Ireland and English-speaking Europe interested in coaching, leadership and wellness, plus a warm retargeting pool of profile visitors and engagers.
  • Dedicated landing page: built and hosted a conversion-focused page for cold traffic, so no changes were needed on the client’s own site.
  • Lead tracking: every DM logged in a shared tracker within 24 hours, measured on attended founder calls and confirmed bookings, not clicks.

The result

Every remaining place on the October retreat booked ahead of the retreat dates.
