Purpose Path

Coaching & walking retreat · Málaga, Spain · Sep 2026

100%Remaining places booked
56Leads from two lead-gen campaigns
€11.35Blended cost per lead
2,651Landing page views at €0.08 each

Selling out a high-ticket retreat with a DM-first Meta funnel.

Overview

Purpose Path is a four-day coaching and walking retreat in the hills above Málaga, run by three coaches. With the October 2026 retreat approaching and places still open, they brought me in on a one-month trial to close the gap before the booking deadline.

The problem

Their Instagram was building trust, but organic reach alone wasn’t filling a €2,599 retreat in time. There was no paid acquisition, no landing page for cold traffic, and no system for tracking which leads actually turned into founder calls and bookings.

The solution

  • DM-first Meta campaign: ran ads on Meta’s Messages objective straight into Instagram DMs, matching the “DM BOOK” call to action the audience already knew, so paid and organic pushed the same behaviour.
  • Four creative angles: the three coaches, a creator’s UGC testimonial, a price-to-value reframe, and a final-week scarcity push. Weak concepts were paused and budget moved to the winners.
  • Tight targeting: 35 to 60 year olds across the UK, Ireland and English-speaking Europe interested in coaching, leadership and wellness, plus a warm retargeting pool of profile visitors and engagers.
  • Dedicated landing page: built and hosted a conversion-focused page for cold traffic, so no changes were needed on the client’s own site.
  • Lead tracking: every DM logged in a shared tracker within 24 hours, measured on attended founder calls and confirmed bookings, not clicks.

Fashion content built to survive the scroll.

Overview

End-to-end TikTok and Instagram management for a fashion brand: content strategy, production and distribution owned start to finish, run through my agency SAVED Marketing. In under two years the account went from a standing start to four TikToks that broke 100K views each.

Creative direction

Fashion content lives or dies on whether it moves. The direction was built around motion and trend fluency: London studio and Canary Wharf location shoots, model-led framing, and styling chosen to read at speed in 9:16. Every shoot was scored to a soundtrack chosen up front, because on TikTok the audio often decides whether a video gets picked up.

Content production

A full pipeline behind every video: location scouting, model casting and communications, call sheets, on-set direction and styling, run inside tight shooting windows so one day produced a batch of content. That repeatable process is what kept a 52-video posting rhythm going without quality dropping.

Distribution

TikTok and Instagram were treated as two different jobs. TikTok carried discovery, with trend- and sound-driven short form built to travel beyond the existing following. Instagram carried retention, with Reels and grid content that converted that discovery into a following.

Pre-production

Every shoot ran from a written pack: run of day, content boards, a behind-the-scenes brief, and locations at Somerset House and a London studio. This is the videographer pack for the 14 December 2024 shoot.

The result

Four flagship TikToks totalling 1.5M+ views (652K, 401K, 329K and 116K), and an Instagram grown from zero to 20K+ followers in two months. The videos are in the Featured content section above.

Pocus Frimley

Healthcare education · Paid lead generation · Republic of Ireland

ROINew market: Republic of Ireland
3Audience motivations, each with its own creative
MetaLead forms into a client lifecycle system
LiveFootage captured inside real training sessions

Reaching doctors, the hardest audience to reach.

Overview

Pocus Frimley teaches doctors and healthcare professionals point-of-care ultrasound. The brief was to build the demand-generation engine behind their expansion into the Republic of Ireland, a market where they had no existing presence or following. The ads promoted a one-day POCUS Taster Day at the Crowne Plaza, Dublin Airport on 3 October 2024: hands-on, expert-led training for GPs and GP trainees, with CPD recognition applied for.

The problem

Doctors are time-poor, resistant to cold outreach and gatekept by reception staff. They are also sceptical by training: a piece of content had to prove it was credible and that the course would concretely help them within about 60 seconds, or it would not be considered. Generic "learn ultrasound" messaging does not survive that.

The approach

  • Three motivations, three creative angles: career advancement, patient-care impact, and belonging to a specialist community of practice, with segmentation by career stage and specialism underneath.
  • Real footage: content captured inside live in-person training sessions, so the ads showed what the course actually looks like in the room.
  • Ireland-only delivery: Meta lead generation geo-targeted to the Republic of Ireland, with a radius exclusion around Dublin and Carlow to keep spend inside the expansion market.
  • Competitor audit: live ads from an existing London private ultrasound provider reviewed through Meta’s Ad Library to see what already worked in the category.
  • Lifecycle follow-up: leads captured through Meta lead forms and routed into a client lifecycle system (attract, engage, convert, retention, loyalty, nurture, grow), so follow-up matched where each lead was in their decision.

Ad creative

Creative for the Dublin Taster Day: real footage from training sessions, clear who-it-is-for copy, and one call to action, book your spot.
